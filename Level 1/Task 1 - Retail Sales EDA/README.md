# Level 1 Task 1 – Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, product performance and revenue distribution.

## Objectives
- Inspect and understand the retail sales dataset.
- Calculate descriptive statistics.
- Analyse monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Examine correlations between numerical variables.
- Generate business recommendations.

## Tools and Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Dataset
The dataset contains 500 retail sales records and 11 original columns, including product details, prices, quantities, order dates, customer demographics and countries.

## Analysis Performed
1. Data inspection and preparation.
2. Descriptive statistical analysis.
3. Monthly and quarterly sales analysis.
4. Customer gender and age-group analysis.
5. Top 10 product performance analysis.
6. Revenue analysis by product category.
7. Correlation heatmap.
8. Sales analysis by country.

## Key Findings
- Total sales revenue: 731,790.
- Laptops generated the highest individual product sales (99,040).
- Accessories was the highest-revenue category (280,575).
- The 46–55 age group contributed the highest sales among age groups (164,691).
- The USA recorded the highest sales by country (138,661).
- Unit price and quantity showed positive correlations with total sales.

## Conclusion
The analysis provided useful insights into retail sales performance, customer demographics and product trends. The findings demonstrate how Python-based data analysis can support business decision-making.
